hush privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

hush is a white-noise app for babies. It collects no personal data.

What hush collects

Nothing. hush has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it never sends anything over the internet.

What stays on your phone

hush remembers your settings (the chosen sound, the distance to the crib, the units, the volume and the sleep timer) so they are there the next time you open it. They are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Play audio in the background, so the sound keeps playing with the screen locked and shows controls on the lock screen.
  • Change audio settings, so hush can read the phone's media volume and keep it under the safe step while it plays.

hush does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or files.

Children

hush is meant to be used by parents and caregivers. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
